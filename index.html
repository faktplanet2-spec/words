About the Project

Forgotten Words is an interactive digital tome and dictionary of archaic, obsolete, and rare words across the great world languages: Russian, English, French, German, Latin, Spanish, Italian, and Old Church Slavonic.

We collect archaisms, historicisms, and forgotten literary treasures from world classical literature, ancient chronicles, and cultural heritage — pairing each with clear modern equivalents.

Every day we feature a new forgotten word, so you can expand your vocabulary and see the world through the eyes of past generations and diverse cultures.
